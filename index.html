About

Building performance into the car, not just watching it.

I'm a software and controls engineer with a Telecommunications Engineering background from UPC Barcelona, currently pursuing a Master's degree in Deep Learning. Motorsport has been a passion since childhood — through Formula 1 and MotoGP — and over the years it developed into a focused interest in the engineering behind high-performance racing cars.

Since September 2024 I've been a Software Developer at NXP TrustMotion (formerly TTTech Auto), developing real-time automotive software for central gateway ECUs in Software-Defined Vehicle architectures — AUTOSAR, Embedded C, CAN, Automotive Ethernet, ECU diagnostics and network routing, in a professional environment where reliability, interface management and disciplined validation are essential.

Before that, two seasons at BCN eMotorsport gave me hands-on ownership of vehicle control systems, state estimation, telemetry and trackside test operation on an AWD electric race car. My telecommunications and signal-processing foundation carries directly into sensor processing, state estimation and real-time control.

P6 Perception

Depth Estimation from 2D+3D Sensor Fusion

InPercept · TSC UPC / Ficosa · Research Assistant · 2023–2024 (BSc thesis)

Built a depth estimator for vehicles ahead using camera and RADAR only, redesigning the dataset's detection-oriented ground truth so the estimator could be evaluated without bias.

Diagnosed a systematic bias in oversized detection boxes; designed an Adaptive Point GT from a cumulative return-distance histogram with a windowed high-pass operator; Mask R-CNN instance segmentation to filter returns to the vehicle contour; defined ME/MAE/RMSE/STD validation with a 60 m validity cut-off.

Outcome: across 118,991 annotations, contour filtering improved every metric — MAE 3.63 → 2.98 m, RMSE 5.09 → 4.41 m — isolating RADAR return density (not the algorithm) as the binding constraint.
